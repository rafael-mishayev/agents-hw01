Personal summary of Dana Levi

I'm Dana, a machine learning engineer based in Tel Aviv. I grew up in Haifa and
moved to Tel Aviv for my master's degree. I have always been drawn to the
point where language and technology meet, which is why I ended up working with
NLP and, more recently, large language models.

What I do best is take a messy, ambiguous problem and turn it into something
measurable. I like to start every project by asking "how will we know it
works?" and building an evaluation before building the model. That habit
came from my years in recommendation systems, where a small improvement on
paper could mean nothing for real users, and it has shaped how I lead my team
at Lumina Health today.

I enjoy mentoring. I teach Python to high-school students and mentor junior
women entering data science, and I find that explaining a concept clearly is
the fastest way to understand it myself. I'm a calm person under pressure,
direct but kind in code reviews, and I prefer simple solutions over clever ones.

Outside of work I like trail running, especially in the Carmel and the Galilee,
and I play the piano casually. I read a lot of science fiction, and my
favorite author is Ursula K. Le Guin. On weekends I often cook for friends,
and I'm slowly trying to master homemade sourdough bread.

Right now I'm interested in making LLM applications more trustworthy:

feedback loops that let a system check and improve its own output. I'm always
happy to talk about NLP, evaluation methods, or career paths in AI.
